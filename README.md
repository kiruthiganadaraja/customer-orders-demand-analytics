# customer-orders-demand-analytics
Exploratory data analysis of a food aggregator's order data to uncover restaurant demand patterns and recommend ways to improve customer experience.
Food Aggregator Order Analysis

A food aggregator company collects data on every order placed by its registered customers through its online portal. The goal of this project is to analyze that order data to identify demand trends across restaurants, cuisines, and customer segments, and to translate those insights into actionable recommendations.

As part of the Data Science team, this analysis answers key business questions around:

Most popular restaurants and cuisines
Order volume patterns across weekdays vs. weekends
Customer rating behavior and its relationship with order cost
Revenue contribution by restaurant
Average delivery and preparation times
Tech stack: Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

Deliverables: Cleaned dataset, EDA notebook, visualizations, and a summary of insights & recommendations to improve customer experience and drive business growth.
